## The Foundations Of Business Strategy 

Hamilton Helmer describes following seven different strategic powers a business can have that sets them apart from others. Hamilton provides examples of business for each power, explaining benefit and barrier of having it.

- Scale Economies:
    As the business scales the cost per unit comes down, giving the business better margins than their competition.

- Network Economies:
    When your business or service lets customers have connection on it's platform, customers are locked-in. Even if one customer wants to move out, rest of the customer still locked-in.

- Counter Positioning:
    When your business model gives you an edge to charge higher or lower price and incumbent does not / can not copy that invalidates their business

- Switching Costs:
    When your customer is invested enough in your product or service eco-system and cost of switching is to another product or service is higher than staying with your business.

- Branding:
    When you've built a enough reputation as a brand that let's you benefit out of it.

- Cornered Resources:
    When the essential resources are exclusive to you, you have an edge that other business cant have.

- Process Power:
    When you have an unique process of producing or delivering better product or service at lower cost.
